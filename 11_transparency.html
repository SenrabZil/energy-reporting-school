The physical market
behind the trades.

Regulation 543/2013 makes the facts of the EU electricity system public: generation, load, transmission, balancing and outages. When a big unit trips, the whole market is meant to see it within the hour.

with Elizabeth Cecilia. A different data chain, from different systems, with its own deadlines.

01

From asset to everyone

The primary owner of the data — a generator, say — passes it to the TSO. The TSO collects and validates it and sends it to the ENTSO-E Transparency Platform, where anyone can read it. In the Netherlands the TSO is TenneT.

This data rarely comes from the trading book. It comes from asset master data, telemetry, outage systems and TSO interfaces — which is why it needs its own controls, separate from the trade reporting ones.

03

Two thresholds that aren’t the same

For generation units of 100 MW or more, unavailability is published as soon as possible and no later than one hour after the change. Below that, there is no per-unit publication duty under Regulation 543/2013. Push the slider past 100 MW and watch the window appear; push the delay past 60 minutes and watch it go late.

REMIT doesn’t use the threshold at all. Inside information is about whether the news would move prices, not how many megawatts it involves. A small unit in a tight hour can still be inside information — and that goes out through an IIP, a different channel with a different legal basis.

04

Disclosure is not transaction reporting

Publishing an outage and reporting a trade both feed market surveillance, but they are different acts with different owners. Transparency data is usually produced by operations; trade reports by the trading and reporting teams. Firms that treat them as one problem tend to miss the lineage of the first.

The 100 MW and one-hour figures are the headline rules for generation units. Which datasets apply to which asset is a question for the firm and its TSO.

05

The rule, in one breath

Units of 100 MW or more: publish an outage within the hour. REMIT asks a different question: does it move the price?

The chain runs from the primary owner to the TSO to the ENTSO-E platform, and it starts in operational systems, not the trading book.
