Lesson 08 · MiFIR

Rebuild the trade
from the report.

MiFIR asks investment firms for a transaction report that lets a regulator reconstruct the trade on its own: who decided, who executed, what, when and where. Many energy traders never file one. Someone else does.

with Elizabeth Cecilia. One decision tree decides who reports. Learn the branches.

01

Sixty-five fields, four questions

MiFIR Article 26 requires investment firms to report each transaction in a financial instrument to their national competent authority by close of the following working day, directly or through an ARM. RTS 22 sets out 65 fields, and they answer four questions.

QuestionAnswered by
Who?Buyer and seller LEIs, and the person or algorithm that decided and the one that executed — reported separately
What?The ISIN, quantity and price
When?The execution date and time
Where?The venue’s MIC, or an off-venue code

03

What the branches mean

An investment firm reports its own transactions. An exempt firm doesn’t — but if it trades on a venue, Article 26(5) makes the venue operator report on its behalf, so the regulator still sees the trade. Off venue, an exempt firm has no MiFIR report at all, although the same trade can still be reportable under EMIR and REMIT.

The LEI toggle is the trap. An investment firm should not execute a transaction for a legal-entity client without a valid LEI: no LEI, no trade. The report can’t be made, so the trade shouldn’t be either.

04

Change watch

The MiFIR review (Regulation 2024/791) amended Article 26. ESMA notes that the amendments apply together with the revised RTS 22, so track that date rather than assuming the new fields are already live.

This shows how the reporting branches work. Whether a firm qualifies for the exemption, or an instrument is in scope, is a legal question the school doesn’t answer.

05

The rule, in one breath

Investment firms report by close of T+1; exempt firms on a venue are reported by the venue; no LEI, no trade.

Sixty-five fields under RTS 22, so a regulator can rebuild the trade alone: who decided, who executed, the ISIN, the time, the venue.
